Privacy Policy

ScaleHackr · Effective date: November 12, 2026

ScaleHackr ("the app") is published by Jeehee Kang ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app works entirely on your device, collects no personal data, and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no artificial-intelligence features, and it does not need an internet connection to work.

2. Information stored only on your device

To remember your work, the app saves the following on your device only. We cannot see it.

WhatWhy
Settings (instrument, theme color, metronome sound, reminders)So the app opens the way you left it
Practice history (ScaleTrackr) and saved scoresTo show your progress
Pen drawings on scores and custom fingeringsTo keep your annotations
Practice recordings you choose to makeSo you can listen back to them
Membership statusTo unlock the features you purchased

You can delete this data at any time by deleting individual items in the app, or by uninstalling the app, which removes all of it.

Backups. In Settings you can save a backup file of this data (and, if you choose, your recordings). The file is created on your device and goes only where you send it, for example iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Files. We never receive it. Membership status is not included in backups.

3. Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you use the tuner or record your practice. Sound is processed on your device in real time to detect pitch, and recordings are saved only on your device. Audio is never uploaded or shared. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your device settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your name, payment card or billing details. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to those transactions. The app receives only a confirmation of what you purchased, which it keeps on your device.

5. Contacting us

If you email us (for example, to report an issue), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you and delete it when it is no longer needed. We never add you to a mailing list.

6. Children

The app is suitable for music students of all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. All app data is on your device and under your control. If you have emailed us and want that message deleted, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and include the new version in an app update.
